Privacy Policy

Version effective as of September 23, 2026

In this privacy policy (this “Privacy Policy”), we, Nadia Elaine Banna and Dr. med. Florian Pfefferkorn (hereinafter, we or us), explain how we collect and otherwise process personal data.

The term “personal data” or “data” refers to all information that relates to a specific or identifiable person. If you provide us with the personal data of other persons (for example, family members or work colleagues), please make sure that these persons are aware of this Privacy Policy and only share their data with us if you are allowed to do so and if this personal data is correct.

Note that other privacy policies or general terms and conditions, conditions of participation, and similar documents may govern specific data privacy topics. This Privacy Policy is aligned with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”) and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (“FADP”). However, the application of these laws depends on each case. The terms used are not gender-specific.

1. Controllers

Nadia Elaine Banna, Uetlibergstrasse 111A, 8045 Zürich, is responsible for the data processing in connection with this website, the contact and appointment forms, and the complementary-therapy services (Alexander Technique), unless otherwise specified in individual instances. If you have any data privacy concerns, you can communicate them to us at the following contact address:

Postal address: Uetlibergstrasse 111A, 8045 Zürich
Email: nadia@nadiabanna.com

Dr. med. Florian Pfefferkorn provides his medical services at the practice of Viva Hub AG, Hohlstrasse 532, 8048 Zürich (email: vivahub@hin.ch). The processing of patient data in connection with these treatments is governed by the Viva Hub AG privacy policy (in German).

2. Collection and Processing of Personal Data

We primarily process the personal data that you provide to us yourself, for example when you contact us, request an appointment or receive treatment from us, as well as data generated when you visit our website. To the extent permitted, we also receive data from third parties, for example from referring doctors, other professionals involved in your care, or your insurer.

This includes in particular:

  • contact and personal details (for example, name, address, telephone number, email address and date of birth);
  • information from inquiries and correspondence (for example, preferred appointment times and the content of your messages);
  • health data, meaning information about your state of health, symptoms, findings and treatments, which counts as sensitive personal data;
  • insurance and billing data (for example, insurer, insurance number, invoices and payments);
  • data in connection with the use of the website (for example, IP address, information about your device and settings, date and time of visit, and pages viewed).

3. Purpose of Data Processing and Legal Grounds

We use the personal data we collect primarily to plan, provide and document your treatment (in particular the practice of medicine and the practice of the Alexander Technique, a complementary-medicine method), to bill our services to you or your insurer, and to comply with our legal obligations, such as the duty to keep patient records.

We also process data, to the extent permitted, for the following purposes, in which we have a legitimate interest:

  • Responding to your inquiries and scheduling appointments;
  • Offering and further developing our services and website;
  • Ensuring that our operations, in particular our IT and website, run properly and securely;
  • Asserting legal claims and defending ourselves in connection with legal disputes and official proceedings.

If you voluntarily share information about your health with us, for example in an inquiry, we process it on the basis of your consent in the context of your inquiry. Patient data is subject to professional confidentiality. We treat it as confidential and only disclose it with your consent or where the law permits or requires it.

4. Website, Forms and Communication

Hosting (GitHub Pages). This website is hosted by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, as a static site on GitHub Pages. When you visit the website, GitHub automatically collects and stores technical access data in server log files (such as your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page requested and your browser type) for the purpose of delivering the website securely and reliably. For more information, see GitHub’s privacy statement: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

No cookies or tracking. This is a static website. We do not set our own cookies, and we do not use analytics, advertising, profiling or social-media tracking tools. We do not create profiles of your browsing behavior, and no third-party plug-ins (such as social-network buttons) are embedded that would transmit data while you browse.

4.1 Contact and Appointment Forms (Formspree)

If you contact us through the contact form or the appointment-request form, the data you enter (such as your name, email address, telephone number, preferred times and message) is transmitted to and processed on our behalf by our form provider Formspree, Inc. (USA), which forwards your message to us by email (see Section 4.2). Your message may contain information about your health, for example when you describe what brings you to us. This information is transmitted the same way. We use this data solely to process and respond to your inquiry. If your inquiry concerns medical services, we forward it to Dr. med. Florian Pfefferkorn. As the data is processed in the United States, the notes in Section 6 apply. For more information, see Formspree’s privacy policy: Formspree Privacy Policy. You can always contact us directly by email or telephone instead of using the forms.

4.2 Email, Telephone and WhatsApp

Our email is hosted by Google. Emails you send us, and messages forwarded from our forms, are therefore stored on Google’s servers, including in the United States. If you contact us via WhatsApp, your messages are transmitted through the WhatsApp service (Meta), and WhatsApp’s own privacy policy also applies. For transfers abroad, see Section 6. If you prefer not to share confidential health information through these channels, please call us or discuss it with us in person.

5. Transfer of Data to Third Parties

We only share data with third parties insofar as this is necessary and permitted for the purposes set out in Section 3. We share patient data only within the limits of professional confidentiality (see Section 3). In particular, the following categories of recipients may be concerned:

  • your health insurer or supplementary insurer, insofar as this is necessary for billing;
  • other professionals involved in your care, in particular Dr. med. Florian Pfefferkorn if you choose a combined approach, as well as referring doctors, in each case with your consent;
  • our service providers, including processors such as IT and website-service providers (for example, GitHub, Formspree and Google, named above) and, where applicable, billing services;
  • authorities or courts, insofar as we are legally required to share data with them;
  • other parties in potential or pending legal proceedings.

With service providers that process data on our behalf, we agree on the data-protection terms required by law, usually through their standard data-processing agreements.

6. Transfer of Data Abroad

When you use our website, our forms and our email address, data is processed in the United States by our hosting provider (GitHub), our form provider (Formspree) and our email provider (Google). If you contact us via WhatsApp, your data may also be transferred to the United States.

Under Swiss law, the United States offers adequate data protection only for companies certified under the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. GitHub and Google are certified under this framework. Formspree relies on the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission (available here). In other cases, we may rely on an exemption provision (for example, your consent, the performance of a contract, or the establishment, exercise or defense of legal claims).

7. Duration of the Retention of Personal Data

We process and retain your data for as long as it is necessary for the fulfillment of our contractual and legal obligations or otherwise for the purposes pursued with the processing. We keep patient records for at least ten years after the end of the last treatment. Inquiries that do not lead to treatment are deleted once they have been dealt with and are no longer needed. Otherwise, data that is no longer required is, as a matter of principle and to the extent possible, deleted or anonymized. For operational data (such as server logs), shorter retention periods of twelve months or less generally apply.

8. Data Security

We take appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access and misuse. We follow best practices for secure data processing and storage, take the protection of personal data into account when designing or choosing hardware, software and processes, and ensure data-protection-friendly default settings.

9. Obligation to Provide Personal Data

In the course of our relationship, you must provide those personal data that are necessary to start and perform the relationship and to fulfill the associated obligations. Without this data, we will generally not be able to enter into or perform an agreement with you. Our website also cannot be used if certain traffic-securing information (such as your IP address) is not disclosed.

10. Profiling and Automated Individual Decision-Making

We do not carry out profiling that has legal effects on you, and in establishing and carrying out our relationship we generally do not use fully automated individual decision-making. Should we use such procedures in certain cases, we will inform you separately where required by law and advise you of your relevant rights.

11. Your Rights

To the extent provided by applicable law (as is the case where the GDPR applies), you have the following rights:

  • the right to request information about whether and what data we process about you;
  • the right to have us correct data if it is inaccurate;
  • the right to request that we delete data;
  • the right to request that we provide certain personal data in a commonly used electronic format or transfer it to another controller;
  • the right to withdraw consent, insofar as our processing is based on your consent;
  • the right to object to the processing of your data.

Please note that we reserve the right to assert the restrictions provided for by law, for example, if we are required to retain or process certain data, or need it for the assertion or defense of claims. To exercise your rights, you may contact us at the address provided in Section 1; we may require you to prove your identity. Every data subject also has the right to file a complaint with the competent data-protection authority. In Switzerland, this is the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (edoeb.admin.ch).

12. Amendments to this Privacy Policy

We may amend this Privacy Policy at any time without prior notice. The current version published on our website shall apply. If this Privacy Policy is part of an agreement with you, we will notify you by email or other appropriate means in the case of an amendment.
