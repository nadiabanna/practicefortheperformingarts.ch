Services

How can we help you?

Find below some of the specific challenges we treat, across conventional medicine and complementary therapy.

Medical Services

Conventional medicine, tailored to artists

Pain Therapy

Personalized, pinpointed relief

Pain therapy is personalized, pinpointed treatment using state-of-the-art methods and an understanding of chronic or acute pain. Dr. med. Florian Pfefferkorn will inform you about his assessment of your case, form a treatment plan and offer a series of pain-therapy treatments, if indicated. If pain therapy isn’t the right solution, he is well-equipped as a doctor of internal medicine to offer a variety of other treatments.

Prevention

Build a strong foundation

Feeling well but want to be sure you stay in good shape? Injury, stress and psychosomatic challenges can appear out of the blue, so it’s worthwhile to build a strong foundation of health. Dr. Pfefferkorn offers consultations on preventative measures for longevity on all fronts: physical and mental health, career and general healthspan, including bloodwork, an examination of current stressors and personalized suggestions.

Complementary Therapy

The Alexander Technique

Your body is your instrument. The Alexander Technique gives you a way to consciously choose how much tension and effort to use, and how to organize mind and body for maximum creative freedom.

Health Optimization

Reach the next level

You’re feeling well and at the top of your game. As a performing artist you’re like an athlete: there’s always another level to reach, with the right team on your side. When optimization is your goal, we tailor-make a plan using functional, evidence-based treatments to bring you right where you want to be.

Postural Strain

Unlock your body’s potential

As a performing artist, your body is extensively used and habits form. The Alexander Technique helps you consciously choose how much tension and effort to use, and how to organize your mind and body for maximum creative freedom.

Anxiety & Stage Fright

Meet yourself with presence

Maybe you’ve always struggled with it, or maybe it arose spontaneously and stuck around. Can you reveal your authentic, full self in front of an audience? With the Alexander Technique, you’ll discover a way to meet yourself with presence and empathy, and dissolve the anxiety that is paralyzing you.

Mind & Body

Freedom of choice

Imagine having freedom of choice in your artistic decisions and performances, a world where you aren’t constrained by technical limitations or habits of movement, breath and thought. Or perhaps you already feel strong and want to go further. In an Alexander Technique session, you’ll find a way to optimize body and mind, wherever your starting point is.
