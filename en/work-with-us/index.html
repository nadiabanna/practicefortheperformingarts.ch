How we work

We combine traditional medicine with complementary therapy, collaborating closely so you get the best of each of our specializations, tailored to you as a person and a performing artist.

The first step

What your first consultation looks like

In the first consultation, we get to know you, form a treatment plan and offer you an initial evaluation. We then discuss your case together as a team, finalize our treatment recommendation and make an appointment for the first treatment.

What’s different from a traditional doctor, or a complementary therapist working alone? We aim to treat the whole of you, as a person and a performing artist, and we collaborate closely to offer you the best of each of our specializations.

Working with us means you get:

  • The latest evidence-based treatments
  • Highly informed practitioners who understand the challenges of a performing arts career
  • A unique collaborative focus that reaches the root cause of your challenges
  • An empathetic, humane and confidential doctor-therapist-client relationship
