Treatment costs

Insurance & costs, made simple

Do I have the right insurance, and will it pay for my treatment? Very likely, yes. Here’s a straightforward guide to sorting it out.

Insurance coverage

Who pays for what

We keep invoicing simple and use the industry-standard tarifs for traditional medicine and complementary therapy. Consult the guide for a quick overview, and reach out anytime with questions.

Medical Services

All medical services are covered and charged under the basic insurance. This means all Swiss basic insurances recognise Dr. Pfefferkorn’s services. A referral may be needed based on your insurance model.

Complementary Therapy

Complementary therapy is covered and charged under the complementary insurance. Nadia Banna is EMR and ASCA recognised, which means her services are covered by almost all complementary insurances. Please check with your complementary insurer to verify the terms of your coverage. A quick way is to call, email or use your insurer’s app and ask about coverage to see Nadia Banna as an Alexander Technique practitioner.

Self-pay patients

It is also possible to pay out of pocket, for example if you don’t reside in Switzerland, or if you don’t have complementary insurance.

Still have questions?

Do you have questions about any of the details regarding insurance coverage? Feel free to send us a message or ask us personally.
